## Description
`Souqira is a modern marketplace for discovering businesses and commercial opportunities across Iraq.

Browse listings by city, explore detailed business information, save favorites, and connect with sellers through a streamlined mobile experience.

With support for English, Arabic, and Kurdish, Souqira is designed for the local market and helps users discover opportunities more easily.

Key features:
- Browse businesses for sale across multiple cities
- Search and filter listings by location and category
- View rich listing details with photos and pricing
- Save favorites for later
- Contact sellers and manage your profile
- Multilingual experience in English, Arabic, and Kurdish

Souqira helps connect buyers, sellers, and partners in one trusted marketplace.`
